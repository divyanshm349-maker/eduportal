Mathematics Notes

Chapter 1: Integers (पूर्णांक)

Integers include all positive and negative numbers along with zero.

  • Addition Rules: (+) + (+) = (+); (-) + (-) = (-)
  • Multiplication: (-) × (-) = (+); (-) × (+) = (-)

Chapter 2: Fractions & Decimals

A fraction represents a part of a whole. Decimals are another way to represent fractions.

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

Chapter 3: Basic Algebra

Algebra uses letters (variables) to represent unknown numbers.

  • Expression: 3x + 5
  • Equation: 3x + 5 = 20 (Solve for x)

Science Notes

Nutrition in Plants

Plants prepare their own food through Photosynthesis.

CO₂ + H₂O + Sunlight → Glucose + O₂

Chlorophyll is the green pigment that captures sunlight.

Acids, Bases and Salts

Acids are sour (e.g., Lemon), Bases are bitter (e.g., Soap).

  • Litmus Test: Acid turns blue litmus red; Base turns red litmus blue.

Motion and Time

Speed is the distance covered per unit time.

Speed = Distance / Time

English Grammar

Parts of Speech

Words are categorized into 8 parts based on their function.

  • Noun: Name of a person, place, or thing.
  • Pronoun: Replaces a noun (He, She, It).
  • Verb: Action word (Run, Eat).
  • Adjective: Describes a noun (Tall, Smart).

Tenses

Tenses indicate the time of action.

  • Present: I go to school.
  • Past: I went to school.
  • Future: I will go to school.

हिंदी व्याकरण

संज्ञा (Noun)

किसी व्यक्ति, वस्तु, स्थान या भाव के नाम को संज्ञा कहते हैं।

  • व्यक्तिवाचक: राम, दिल्ली
  • जातिवाचक: लड़का, शहर

संधि (Sandhi)

दो वर्णों के मेल से उत्पन्न विकार को संधि कहते हैं।

विद्या + आलय = विद्यालय (दीर्घ संधि)

History & Civics

Ancient India

The Indus Valley Civilization was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations.

  • Major cities: Harappa, Mohenjo-daro.
  • They had advanced drainage systems.

Indian Constitution

The Constitution is the supreme law of India. It lays down the framework of fundamental political principles.

Geography

Our Environment

Environment includes all living and non-living things around us.

  • Biotic: Plants, Animals, Humans.
  • Abiotic: Air, Water, Soil.

Earth's Structure

The Earth is made of three layers: Crust, Mantle, and Core.

Computer Science

Basics of Computers

A computer is an electronic device that processes data.

  • Input Devices: Keyboard, Mouse.
  • Output Devices: Monitor, Printer.
  • CPU: The brain of the computer.
